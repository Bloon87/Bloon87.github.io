📖 INFO

Everything the level-up screen can offer. ★ Haste, Caliber and Vitality come up somewhat more often (Haste a bit less than the other two) and every level-up offers at least one. Percentages are the odds of each tier once that upgrade is rolled (before Luck).

▶ v2.6 — Current
• Choco's Dying SMP is now its own thing: a Minecraft-style pixel sword (cookie blade, gold guard, heart pommel), a blocky pixel-spray swing trail, hits that shatter into pixel blocks, and Lifesteal SMP kills — every enemy the sword kills steals a heart-fragment (+4 HP, up to 3 per swing).
• Pause menu now lists every upgrade you have taken (bottom-right corner), coloured by your best rarity, with the count.
• Skyblock Adict: the weapon auto-ability cooldown is cut by 0.5s on every kill (so it fires constantly in big crowds); the cooldowns (AUTO / SPC / R) now show under MP. Terminator has a new bow sprite, all of its damage is -66%, and each volley's damage is split 70% centre arrow / 15% each side arrow.
• The high score is now HIGHEST LEVEL (shown on the main menu and on the game-over screen); it saves the moment you level up.
▶ v2.5
• Skyblock Addict now has its own swords instead of reusing Choco's: Hyperion is a long ice-cyan blade with a gold winged guard, glowing runes, a star glint and a razor-thin crescent slash with cyan sparks; Dark Claymore is a wide jagged void-steel blade with a pulsing soul-gem, a dark violet-and-pink flame crescent and rising embers. Both have their own hit effects and held-sword look.
• All abilities polished + faster: every SPACE / R / ultimate now has a cast shockwave and spark burst. Projectile glows no longer use shadow blur (soft cached glow sprites instead), sparks are glow sprites, bullets and blood are drawn in a handful of batched calls. Minigun now has muzzle bloom, a light cone and a heating barrel; Noglin's latch leaves a neon trail and detonates on capture; Glitter Bomb and Death Note glow; Blizzard adds a frost vignette and wind streaks. Heavy scenes (spinbot, Gates of Babylon) run roughly 20-25% faster.
• Visual polish pass (all cached or batched, no measurable frame cost): the arena now has a lit tile floor, grid, centre sigil, corner glows and a starfield; soft ground shadows under the player and every enemy; a subtle screen vignette; rings are now soft additive glows (and cheaper, no more shadow blur); the Obsessed's Ea, dance and Gates of Babylon got a full VFX upgrade.
• Spinbot buffed: 2.5x damage per bullet and stronger homing. Gates of Babylon: blades now show hit sparks, blood and rolling damage numbers (it was doing damage but looked like it wasn't), bosses show "BOSS RESISTS" at their cap, every enemy gets a gold lock ring, and damage against regular enemies was reduced. Obsession Dance: text is now "NO NO, NO, NO, / YOU DON'T DO THAT!" slammed in word by word, damage pulses are 2.5x stronger, with a disco floor, spotlights and orbiting hearts.
• NEW CHARACTER: The Obsessed 🤩 (BlahBlahBlah variant — unlocked by reaching level 20 with BlahBlahBlah). SPACE is a three-step chain: Kamen Rider Kick (freeze for 0.6s, then dash across the map at the strongest enemy, damaging everything on the path and where you land) → Assault Boost (three long, steerable stunning dashes toward your mouse, 0.5s apart; you can keep moving and shooting throughout) → Ea (2s charge, then a huge radiant golden explosion; the full cooldown only starts after it). R = Viral Obsession Dance ("NONONO YOU CANT DO THAT!" on screen, damage pulses around you). F = Gates of Babylon: 12 golden portals clumped together at the top centre fire 60 weapons per second each for 8 seconds, cycling through the enemies so each gets an equal share (~5,800 knives, saws, swords, daggers, shovels and blades) with low per-hit damage (reduced again in this build), visible hit numbers and a hard cap on what bosses can lose (28%, Mega 11%); the weapons are small blades. The weapon swarm runs on a typed-array pool with a spatial grid, so it costs almost nothing per frame.
• NEW CHARACTER: Skyblock Adict ⛏️ (Coolioioio variant — unlocked by reaching level 20 with Coolioioio). MP: +1 automatically for every 3 upgrades you take, and every MP adds +1.5 damage. SPACE = God Potion (+20 MP for 12s, the cooldown is paused while it lasts). R = WD Swap between Hyperion (bonus mobility, light melee), Dark Claymore (+4 damage, slow heavy melee) and Terminator (bonus pierce and velocity, a bow that fires 3 arrows at once). Each weapon auto-casts an ability every 10s that upgrades at 3 / 6 / 9 MP — Hyperion: sword beam → teleport slash → impact explosion → heal + decaying shield; Claymore: heavy cleave → combo ending with a sword dropped on your cursor → wider splash on every hit → much bigger area damage; Terminator: ignite arrows → exploding arrows → periodic beam shots. F = a different ultimate per weapon: Wither Impact (siphon then explode, huge decaying overshield), Dark Slashes (3 stunning slashes, the last launches every enemy into the walls) and Arrow Storm (homing arrows that explode, ignite and confuse).
• Mega Boss now arrives after every 5 normal bosses you kill (was every 10 levels) and gets 6 new attacks: Bullet Rain, Bloom, Thunder Pillars, Mines, Seekers and Ricochet. Its Meteor Rain is buffed — faster, up to 3 per beat, many land on you — and a hit now gives only 12 i-frames instead of a full second, so the other meteors actually count.
• Balance: poison damage −75%. Skyblock Adict nerfed: +1.5 damage per MP (was +2), Claymore +4 (was +5), God Potion lasts 12s (was 15s), Hyperion mobility reduced, and roughly 25–30% less damage on every weapon, auto-ability, ultimate and its overshield/heal.
• Settings are saved — difficulty, particles, targeting modes, auto-pause and hitbox view survive a refresh.
• Damage-over-time rules: poison tick = 25% of (5 + your DPS + 5% of the victim\'s max HP (1% on Mega Bosses)) — poison was nerfed by 75%; bleed = 10% max HP over 5s (5% on Mega Bosses); burn = 140% of your DPS per second.
• Femboy's Twirl nerfed: no i-frames and no longer destroys projectiles. Charm has been removed from the game: Femboy's ultimate (Gender Reveal) now confuses every regular enemy instead, and the Summoner's Summon Horde (Noglin mind control) now spawns ten confused minions that brawl.
• Pickle-Astrophe: the ultimate floods the whole screen with green brine and tints it, and pickles now fall on the strongest enemy (not a random one), poisoning it and its neighbours.
• Snowman: icicles now leave enemies BRITTLE; the Giant Snowball lasts longer and grows bigger (up to 390px) and deals 20% max HP to Mega Bosses. Coolioioio's 360-No-Scope lines now keep pivoting to stay on their target while you move. God-mode Death Note no longer works on Mega Bosses. Pikachu's "plasma ball" is now consistently called Electroball.
▶ v2.4
• NEW: MEGA BOSS — every 10 levels all spawning stops (enemies and the normal boss timer). Clear the field and THE CHAOS WARDEN arrives: 3 phases, 10 varied attacks (spiral arms, aimed fans, jump slam, rotating death beams, lane charges, meteor rain, bullet curtains, summons, gravity well, blink ambush) with telegraphs. Immune to mind control, charm, stun, fear and confusion. It ONLY arrives when nothing else is alive: the exceptions are a mind-controlled regular (non-boss) unit and charmed units. If the last enemy is the boss Noglin is possessing, Noglin is thrown off it so you have to kill it first
• Femboy's TWIRL reworked: 3s fully-invulnerable pirouette whose sparkle ring grows from 170 to 260px, pulses every 8 frames (≥2× damage, 1% max HP on regular enemies, 0.15% on bosses), pulls regular enemies in and wipes enemy bullets, then ends with a Heartbreak Nova (310px knockback, stun, a big hit and a heart back). New look: spiral ribbon arms, orbiting hearts and a collapsing nova warning, and Femboy actually spins. The Gender Reveal ult still uses a weaker Twirl without the nova.
• Optimisations: Coolioioio's minigun re-picks its target every 3 frames (manual aim stays instant), simulates embers in the game step and draws them in two batched fills; Twirl/Glitter Bomb do their targeting only on pulse frames; enemies no longer allocate a target object every frame.
• Femboy: Slay is gone — SPACE is now GLITTER BOMB (lob a sparkle bomb at your target/cursor: AoE blast, confuses regular enemies, leaves a glitter field). Charm (his ultimate) was fixed: charmed enemies used to just walk at you; now they hunt the nearest un-charmed enemy, fight it on contact and zap it from range. The ultimate is renamed GENDER REVEAL and ends with a massive blue explosion. Twirl/ult visuals are lighter
• Zanther: Sheer Aura is gone — R is now FURY SWIPES, an AoE flurry of three claw waves that scratches everything within 250px and leaves it BLEEDING (new damage-over-time status, 6s, dripping-blood visual). Cooldown 15s (was 30s)
• Zanther's Pounce looks better (arcing leap with afterimages, landing shockwave, pulsing prey ring with a draining feast timer, claw bites + scratch marks) and when the victim dies early he now jumps onto the strongest enemy within reach and keeps feasting (at least 2.5s more); the Frenzy bonus comes when the chain ends
• Feared enemies now flee smartly: they no longer back into a wall or corner and sit there — they pick a route to the open side of the arena, even looping past you at a safe distance to get further away. The pounce's fear radius is also much smaller (200px), so there is still prey in range for the jump
• Apex Frenzy damage reduced: −60% on regular enemies (3.2% max HP per volley), −30% on bosses (0.77%)
• Coolioioio's 360-No-Scope is more interactive: the AIM line snaps exactly to your cursor (no enemy locking, zero lag), the two sub-lines hunt the STRONGest (highest HP) and the FARthest enemy — always two different targets, re-picked live while tracking. Each line is labelled, and pressing R again locks the shot early.
• Noglin's ultimate is now HIVE MIND (replaces UWU Apocalypse), a brain-slug epidemic like the original ARK parasite: everything near you is infected and the infection spreads to the nearest clean enemy every quarter second. Infected enemies are dazed, eaten by the parasite 3 times a second, and burst into slugs when they die (hurting and infecting those around them); bosses can't be dazed but take ×3 damage for 5s. After 8 seconds the hive tears out of every survivor for 30% of its max HP. Still grants +150 shield and a 100 HP heal. Far stronger than the old ult.
• Shiba's Money Rain buffed: lasts 7.5s (was 6), twice as many coins, half of them dropped on enemies, bigger blasts (92px / 140px for big ones) with ~45% more damage, more bonus XP, and a bigger, harder JACKPOT slam (640px reach, 45% of regular enemies' HP).
• XP orbs can no longer spawn or sit outside the playfield (they are clamped to the arena).
• Pikachu's ultimate is now ELECTROBALL (replaces Thunder God): a slow electroball drifts after your cursor for 8 seconds and zaps lightning four times a second — each strike hits the nearest enemy within 430px and arcs to up to 3 more (a nerfed Thunderbolt: ~3.5× damage, short stun on regular enemies). You are only invulnerable for the first second.
▶ v2.3
• Splitter reworked: now a chance to fire 2 extra bullets — Common +10%, Rare +20%, Legendary +40% (max 80%). Legendary also guarantees a double-split at least every 3rd shot; the guarantee is separate from the chance, so owning several Legendaries never changes it. Choco's towers use their own counter.
• Velocity cap raised to +300% (×4). Player bullets now use swept hit detection, so even ×4 bullets can't skip over an enemy.
• Menu reorganised: Keybinds moved into Settings (which is now grouped into Gameplay / Display & System / Keybinds). New 📖 INFO button holds four tabs — Upgrades, Enemies, Abilities and Changelog. The old separate buttons and the Export button (and its save download) are gone.
• Noglin: Delatch removed. While latched, SPACE is the host's special ability and R is Rip: it kills any normal host (with the usual heal and overheal shield) and on a boss instantly deals 50% of its max HP + 4 seconds of your gun DPS, then ejects you with the heal and shield. It can never kill a boss outright (it leaves 1 HP at least). The 60s timer now simply lets a boss go.
• Ability Target setting is now meaningful: SMART (each ability picks what suits it — clusters for Tar Toss / Singularity / Snowball, busiest lane for Stairs Throw, nearest for the rest; 360-NoScope is the exception — its main line is always your cursor), NEAREST, STRONGEST (boss first, then highest HP) or CURSOR. It now drives every targeted ability and ultimate, including Word Barrage, 50 Bucks, Volt Tackle, the Spinbot and the Snowball.
• Enemy catalogue now lists all six boss types (Charger, Turret, Swirler, Bursty, Ghost, Summoner) with their attack pattern, every boss that uses it, and the Noglin special you get from it.
• Starred upgrades (Haste, Caliber, Vitality) show up less: 1.6× as often as others instead of 2.5×, and Haste is rarer than the other two on top of that. Every level-up still offers at least one starred upgrade.
• Main menu box is much wider (up to 1040px) with 4 characters per row on large screens.
• Coolioioio: 360-No-Scope now works like the sniper owls' laser — up to three tracking lines (main target, closest, furthest) follow their targets, lock with a flash, then fire an instant piercing beam along the line, hitting everything on it (regular enemies executed, bosses take 18× bullet damage). The main line is aimed with your cursor (the other two hunt the closest and furthest enemy). Its cooldown starts after the shot.
• Fixed: Zanther's pounce left the victim stunned for up to ~16s after you cancelled it or it timed out — bosses in particular stayed frozen. Bosses are now released instantly (other enemies get a short stagger), and only enemies on the playfield can be pounced.
• Removed the GLORP and CRIT popups. Blah: Word Barrage damage −20%, and the R ability is renamed Cringe Unskippable Monologue. Choco: Dying SMP sword no longer stuns or knocks enemies back.
